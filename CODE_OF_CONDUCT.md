# Community Code of Conduct

FreeDSH aims to be a practical, welcoming open-source community for people experimenting with DeepSeek Harness, model routing and low-cost/free AI infrastructure.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and technically constructive.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good faith, while still asking for evidence when claims are technical.
- Keep bug reports reproducible and provider claims time-stamped when possible.
- Welcome beginners and explain project-specific assumptions.
- Respect privacy and never repost another person's credentials, private logs or personal information.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats or discriminatory attacks.
- Deliberate disruption of issues, pull requests or discussions.
- Publishing private information or secrets.
- Spam, deceptive promotion or fabricated benchmarks.
- Pressuring contributors to violate provider terms or bypass access controls.

## Technical disagreement

Disagreement is expected. When evidence conflicts, prefer reproducible tests, logs, source references and small experiments over authority or rhetoric.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock or remove content and may restrict participation when necessary to protect the project and its community. Repeated or severe violations can result in exclusion from project spaces.

For sensitive conduct or security matters, avoid posting confidential details publicly.
