## What counts as a strong signal?

Higher-value signals:

- someone successfully installs and returns;
- someone files a reproducible provider report;
- someone asks a technical question that improves docs;
- someone opens a PR;
- an outside contributor completes a `good first issue`;
- users compare routing/provider behavior with dated evidence.

Lower-value signals when isolated:

- impressions;
- likes;
- stars with no subsequent usage;
- generic “cool project” comments.

Use the data to decide where to invest promotion time and which onboarding problems need engineering work.
